What Entrepreneurship Means Here

Entrepreneurship is not just business creation. It is:

  • Problem-solving in the real world
  • Turning ideas into action
  • Learning how to build value for people
  • Developing discipline, clarity, and execution skills
  • Understanding failure as part of growth

Core Entrepreneurial Skills

Thinking Skills

  • Problem identification
  • Opportunity spotting
  • Creative ideation
  • Critical thinking

Execution Skills

  • Building and testing ideas
  • Basic product development
  • Project structuring
  • Time and task management

Communication Skills

  • Pitching ideas clearly
  • Storytelling for business
  • Persuasion and influence
  • Networking mindset

Business Understanding

  • Value creation
  • Customer understanding
  • Basic marketing thinking
  • Revenue models & logic

Personal Skills

  • Discipline & consistency
  • Confidence in uncertainty
  • Emotional resilience
  • Decision making under pressure

4-Week Curriculum Breakdown

Week 1

Mindset & Foundation

Reprogramming how you view problems, failure, and value creation.

Week 2

Ideation & Selection

Frameworks for finding, validating, and selecting real opportunities.

Week 3

Micro-Execution

Building the absolute smallest possible version of your idea.

Week 4

Pitch & Iterate

Telling the story, gathering real feedback, and defining next steps.
