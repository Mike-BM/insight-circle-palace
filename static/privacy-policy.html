Privacy Policy

Insight Circle Palace values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights as a member of our community.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register for an account, RSVP for Insighters Monday, enroll in learning programs, or submit an inquiry:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, telephone number.
  • Account credentials: Password hashes and login security records.
  • Payment information: Transaction confirmation references (e.g., M-Pesa codes) submitted for administrative verification.
  • Academic progression: Assignment submissions, quiz responses, attendance records, and generated certificates.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is utilized strictly to provide, improve, and secure our educational services:

  • To authenticate your identity and deliver personalized member access.
  • To process workshop RSVPs and transmit calendar invitations and session links.
  • To verify manual payment submissions and grant program entitlements.
  • To send important announcements, certificate credentials, and password recovery emails.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information is shared only with trusted infrastructure providers necessary to operate the platform (such as cloud hosting, email delivery networks, and meeting platforms), or when required by applicable law.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For questions or privacy requests, please contact us at Insightcirclepalace@gmail.com.
